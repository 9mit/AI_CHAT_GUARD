Privacy Policy & Confidentiality Guarantee

Your strategic ideas, proprietary data, and conversational prompts belong to you alone. Here is our architectural guarantee.

1. 100% Client-Side Processing Paradigm

DeckMind AI compiles presentations entirely in your browser's local sandbox memory. At no point are your prompts, chat transcripts, or generated slides transmitted to any external server, cloud backend, or third-party analytical service.

2. In-Memory Credential & Secret Sanitization

Before analyzing chat turns or assembling slide decks, DeckMind executes a client-side regex scrubber targeting high-risk patterns (AWS Access Keys, OpenAI/Anthropic API Tokens, GitHub PATs, Google API Keys, and JWT credentials). All detected secrets are masked with deterministic redaction tags (e.g. [REDACTED_OPENAI_KEY]) in memory.

3. Minimal Manifest V3 Permissions

DeckMind AI adheres to Chrome's principle of least privilege. We only request:
• activeTab: To read the conversation in the current tab when triggered.
• storage: To save your selected presentation theme and slide length preferences locally.
• contextMenus: To enable right-click generation actions.
We do not request background browser history or identity permissions.

4. Optional Local Open-Source Models

If you choose to connect local Ollama (localhost:11434) or Stable Diffusion WebUI (localhost:7860), all requests remain strictly on your local network (localhost). No external internet traffic is ever generated.

5. Chrome Web Store Compliance

DeckMind AI is strictly audited and compliant with Google Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies. We do not monetize personal data, inject advertisements, or sell user telemetry.
