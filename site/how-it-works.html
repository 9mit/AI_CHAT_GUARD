Quickstart & Interaction Guide

How DeckMind AI Works

From conversational DOM ingestion to full-fidelity PowerPoint compilation in your browser sandbox.

Step 1

Install & Pin Extension

Install DeckMind AI from the Chrome Web Store. Pin the icon to your toolbar for instant popup access.

Step 2

Chat on Any AI Platform

Open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, or Perplexity. DeckMind automatically recognizes active chat turns.

Step 3

Press Alt + Shift + P

Use the global keyboard shortcut or click the floating HUD pill to compile the presentation instantly.

Step 4

Customize in Deck Studio

Press Alt + Shift + D to open the full interactive 3-panel presentation studio to polish slides and themes.
