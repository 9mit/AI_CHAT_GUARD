Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers to common questions about DeckMind AI, privacy, and PowerPoint compatibility.

How do I convert a ChatGPT conversation into PowerPoint?

Install DeckMind AI from the Chrome Web Store. Whenever you are on ChatGPT (or Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek), a discreet "DeckMind AI" action pill appears. Click "PPTX" or press Alt + Shift + P to instantly download a finished 16:9 presentation file.

Does DeckMind AI send my chat data to an external server?

No. DeckMind AI is engineered on a zero-trust model. All scraping, topic extraction, slide generation, and file compilation happen 100% inside your browser's local sandbox using PptxGenJS. Nothing is sent to third-party servers.

Can I edit the generated PowerPoint files?

Yes. The generated .pptx files are standard OpenXML presentations. Every title, bullet point, table, card container, and speaker note can be edited directly inside Microsoft PowerPoint, Apple Keynote, or Google Slides.

Does it support local open-source models like Ollama?

Yes! In DeckMind AI Settings, you can configure local Ollama (http://localhost:11434) or Stable Diffusion WebUI (http://localhost:7860) to generate custom slide graphics and summaries completely offline.
