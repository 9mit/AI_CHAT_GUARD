Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DeckMind AI, privacy guarantees, platform support, and presentation formatting.

What is DeckMind AI?

DeckMind AI is a Manifest V3 Chrome Extension that converts conversational threads from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, and other AI chat interfaces into executive-grade PowerPoint presentations, Word documents, Excel tables, and diagrams with 100% zero-trust client-side privacy.

How is DeckMind AI different from other AI presentation tools?

Unlike tools that require copying text to a third-party cloud website or installing expensive monthly add-ins, DeckMind AI runs directly inside your browser on the active chat tab, has 100% zero cloud transmission, redacts secrets automatically, cites exact turn indices, and exports native 16:9 .pptx files in 1 click.

Can I edit the generated PowerPoint (.pptx) file?

Yes. DeckMind AI outputs native OpenXML Microsoft PowerPoint (.pptx) files. Every title, card, text box, table, and speaker note can be edited directly in Microsoft PowerPoint, Apple Keynote, or Google Slides.

Which AI platforms are supported?

DeckMind AI supports ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, AI Studio, DeepSeek, Perplexity AI, Microsoft Copilot, xAI Grok, Mistral Le Chat, Meta AI, and localhost Open-WebUI / Ollama.

Is DeckMind AI free?

Yes, DeckMind AI is a free Chrome Extension with no hidden subscriptions or watermarks.

How does DeckMind AI protect my privacy?

DeckMind AI operates under a 100% zero-trust local architecture. All parsing and PPTX compilation happen in your browser's local memory. No chat data is ever transmitted to commercial cloud servers or external telemetry databases.
